# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the BeamZ community to be welcoming, respectful, and
free from harassment. This applies to project spaces and to public interactions
representing the project.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and
focus discussion on ideas and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, and the sharing of private information without permission are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to quentin.wach+beamz@gmail.com. Maintainers will
review reports promptly and may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.
